**Job title:** Junior Accountant

**Job description:** Supports the accounts team with invoicing, bookkeeping, expense checks and client queries. Uses Excel daily. Reports to the Accounts Manager.

**Company description:** BrightBooks Accounting is a small accounting firm in Doha with 12 staff. It handles bookkeeping, payroll and VAT preparation for around 80 small and medium businesses, mostly restaurants, retail shops and construction subcontractors. Clients expect fast, accurate answers and plain-language explanations.

**Company policies for this role:**
1. Any invoice or payment above QAR 5,000 must be approved by the Accounts Manager before it is processed.
2. Client financial information is confidential. Never share one client's figures with another client, and never send financial data to a personal email address.
3. Expense claims need a receipt. Claims without a receipt are returned to the employee, not paid.
4. Mistakes found in a client's books must be reported to the Accounts Manager the same day, with a short note of what was found.
5. Reply to client emails within one working day, in a polite and clear tone, without accounting jargon.
