**Job title:** Operations Assistant

**Job description:** Helps the operations team track deliveries, handle delayed or lost parcel cases, update customers and keep daily delivery reports accurate.

**Company description:** Swift Parcels is a same-day and next-day delivery company serving online shops and individual customers across Qatar. It runs 40 drivers and handles about 3,000 parcels a day. Customers track parcels by tracking number, and online shops are the company's most important business customers.

**Company policies for this role:**
1. A parcel is "delayed" after 24 hours past its promised delivery time and "lost" after 5 working days with no scan.
2. Customers with a delayed parcel get an update and a new delivery estimate within 2 hours of contacting us.
3. Lost-parcel claims up to QAR 500 can be approved by an Operations Assistant. Claims above QAR 500 go to the Operations Supervisor.
4. Never share a customer's phone number or address with anyone other than the assigned driver.
5. Every case must be logged in the case tracker with the tracking number, the issue and the action taken.
