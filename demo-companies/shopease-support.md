**Job title:** Customer Support Agent (chat and phone)

**Job description:** Answers customer questions by live chat and phone, handles late deliveries, damaged items and refund requests, and escalates cases that are above an agent's limits. Logs every conversation in the support system. Reports to the Support Team Lead.

**Company description:** ShopEase Qatar is an online store selling electronics and home appliances (TVs, phones, blenders, washing machines) to households across Qatar. Most customers order on the website or app and pay online. Many contact support when a delivery is late or an item arrives damaged, and they expect a quick, friendly answer.

**Company policies for this role:**
1. Refunds are allowed within 14 days of delivery, with proof of purchase (an order number).
2. Support agents can approve refunds up to QAR 500. Refunds above QAR 500 go to the Team Lead.
3. Before sharing any order details (address, items, payment status), verify the customer's email address and order number.
4. Escalate to the Team Lead when an angry customer asks for a manager.
5. Use a friendly, patient tone, and tell customers when they will hear back.
