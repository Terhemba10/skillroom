Hire graduates on what they can do.

Most first jobs ask for experience a graduate can't have yet. SkillRoom gives them real tasks from your company, a short interview about their answers, and gives you a scored report. You still make the call.

See how to use it View the code

"We need two years of experience" stops capable graduates before anyone sees their work.

A CV shows where someone studied. It doesn't show whether they can calm an angry customer, catch a payment over the limit, or reconcile a bank statement. SkillRoom tests exactly that, using your company's own activities and rules.

Three agents, one hiring manager, one graduate.

Everything happens in team chat, connected through Switch. Two linked rooms keep the scoring guide away from the candidate.

  1. 1

    Assessor writes the test

    The hiring manager posts the role, the company and its policies. The Assessor drafts 3 timed tasks set inside that company, plus a scoring guide. Nothing reaches the graduate until the manager approves.

    Hiring room, then interview room

  2. 2

    Interviewer asks about their answers

    A live text interview of about 6 questions. It starts with the graduate's own written answers, then new scenarios, with one follow-up when an answer is vague.

    Interview room

  3. 3

    Evaluator scores, the human decides

    Scores for job knowledge, problem-solving, communication and policy compliance, each with evidence. Plus timing, integrity notes and a suggested next step.

    Hiring room only

Fair by design. Cheating made pointless.

We don't watch candidates with spyware. We make copying useless and leave every judgement to a person.

Unique, company-specific tasks

Names, amounts and dates change for every graduate, and tasks use your real rules, so there's nothing generic to look up or share.

The interview checks the written work

Graduates explain their own answers. People who didn't write them usually can't.

Timed tasks

Each task has a time limit. Timestamps are recorded and late answers are flagged in the report.

Warning signs, not accusations

Style changes, generic AI-like answers or odd timing are noted as observations with a Low, Some or High concern level for a human to review.

Substance over polish

Spelling and English as a second language don't lower scores unless the job is mainly writing.

Policies are provided

New graduates can't know internal rules yet, so each task includes the guidelines it tests. We measure whether they can read a rule and apply it.

How to use it

Always start your message with the agent's name, for example @skillroom-assessor, so it knows the message is for it.

Hiring manager

  1. In skillroom-hiring, post the job title, company description, policies for the role and the graduate's name.
  2. Review the 3 timed tasks and scoring guide the Assessor drafts.
  3. Reply APPROVE, or say what to change.
  4. When the interview ends, read the Evaluator's report and the full transcript, then decide.

Graduate

  1. In skillroom-interview, read the honesty statement and reply I agree.
  2. Answer each task in your own words before its time runs out. Use the company guidelines shown with it.
  3. Answer the Interviewer's questions about your answers, one at a time.
  4. That's it. The hiring team gets your results.
