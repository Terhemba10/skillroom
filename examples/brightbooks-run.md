# Example run: BrightBooks Accounting, Junior Accountant

This was a real test run of SkillRoom on the Windows setup described in [WINDOWS-SETUP.md](../WINDOWS-SETUP.md), on 4 November 2026. The messages below were copied from the two Mattermost rooms. Times are UTC, from the chat's own message timestamps. We left out short status lines like "Worked for 25s" and anything unrelated to this candidate.

**Please note:**
- **Amina Yusuf is a fictional demo candidate.** Her answers were written by the builder's AI assistant playing the graduate, to test the workflow. They are not a real person's work.
- **This run did not reach the final report.** The interview stopped after question 2, and the Evaluator's machine went offline before scoring. What happened, and what we changed because of it, is at the end.

### Task 3

**skillroom-assessor** · 20:36:08

> **Task 3 of 3: Find out why the bank and the books don't agree** (about 10 minutes)
>
> You are checking October records for **Marina Corner Mini-Mart** (a retail client). On 31 October 2026:
> - Cash balance in the client's books (ledger): **QAR 48,960**
> - Balance on the bank statement: **QAR 44,575**
>
> While checking, you find:
> a) A bank charge of **QAR 85** on the statement that is not in the books.
> b) A card-sales deposit of **QAR 3,400** recorded in the books on 31 Oct, which only reaches the bank on 2 Nov.
> c) The October shop rent was paid by bank for **QAR 6,500** but was entered in the books as **QAR 5,600**.
>
> **Step by step, explain:** 1. Why the two balances are different, and how each item affects them. 2. What the correct cash balance at 31 October is. 3. What you would do next, and who you would tell.
>
> > **Company guidelines:** Policy 4: "Mistakes found in a client's books must be reported to the Accounts Manager the same day, with a short note of what was found."

**Graduate** · 20:39:33

> 1. Why the two balances are different
> The books show QAR 48,960 and the bank shows QAR 44,575, a difference of QAR 4,385. Three items explain it:
> a) Bank charge QAR 85: the bank has taken it, but it isn't in the books yet. The books are QAR 85 too high.
> b) Card-sales deposit QAR 3,400: recorded in the books on 31 Oct, but it only reaches the bank on 2 Nov. This is a timing difference (deposit in transit). The bank balance is QAR 3,400 lower for now, and nothing is wrong in the books.
> c) Shop rent: the bank paid QAR 6,500, but the books recorded QAR 5,600. This is a data-entry mistake (the digits were swapped). The books are QAR 900 too high.
>
> Check: 85 + 3,400 + 900 = 4,385, which matches the difference.
>
> 2. Correct cash balance at 31 October
> Books: 48,960 - 85 (bank charge) - 900 (rent correction) = QAR 47,975
> Bank: 44,575 + 3,400 (deposit in transit) = QAR 47,975
> Both sides agree, so the correct cash balance is QAR 47,975.
>
> 3. What I would do next and who I would tell
> - Post two adjustments in the books: record the QAR 85 bank charge, and correct the rent from QAR 5,600 to QAR 6,500.
> - Note the QAR 3,400 deposit as in transit and check on the November statement that it arrived on 2 Nov.
> - Following Policy 4, report the rent entry mistake to the Accounts Manager today, with a short note: "Marina Corner Mini-Mart, October: rent entered as QAR 5,600 instead of QAR 6,500 (books overstated by QAR 900). Correction prepared for your review. Also added a QAR 85 bank charge. Adjusted balance QAR 47,975, matches the bank after the QAR 3,400 deposit in transit."
> - I wouldn't contact the client about the error myself until the Accounts Manager has reviewed it.

> In Task 2 you decided not to release Mr. Al-Kuwari's QAR 7,450 payment, but you did prepare it so it was ready. Walk me through why you chose to prepare it rather than just leave it for Ms. Farouk, and what you would say if he called back and insisted his crew will walk off on Saturday.

**Graduate** · 20:47:09

> I prepared it rather than just leaving it because the payment may well be genuine and urgent. If it's ready, Ms. Farouk only needs to check and approve it when she leaves her meeting at 18:00, so we lose as little time as possible without breaking the approval rule.
>
> If Mr. Al-Kuwari called back and insisted his crew would walk off on Saturday, I would stay calm and understanding, but I still wouldn't release it myself. I'd tell him I understand the pressure, that the payment is fully prepared, and that I'm escalating it right now. Then I would try to reach Ms. Farouk directly with a short message or call ("Urgent: SUB-8812, QAR 7,450, client says crew will walk off Saturday, needs your approval"). If she couldn't be reached, I'd ask whoever covers for her or the most senior person available what to do. The approval limit exists to protect the client and the firm, especially when there is time pressure and a request to use a personal email address, which can be signs of fraud.

## 4. What happened next, and what we changed

The run stopped here, for three reasons:

1. **The Evaluator's machine went offline.** At 20:43:51 Switch replied on the Evaluator's behalf: *"My machine, Terhemba, is offline or reconnecting to Switch, so I can't answer right now."* The Assessor noticed and told the manager in the hiring room. Three agents, Mattermost and Docker were all running on one laptop. We later added "give each reply 1–3 minutes" and the restart fixes in [WINDOWS-SETUP.md](../WINDOWS-SETUP.md#troubleshooting).
2. **A second graduate was started in the same room.** During Amina's interview, the tester posted a new Customer Support role (for "Yusuf Bello") in the interview room. The Assessor asked for it to go to the hiring room instead, so the candidate wouldn't see the scoring guide. It then started Yusuf's tasks in the same room and addressed each message by name to keep the two apart. That worked, but it was messy. We added a rule: **one graduate at a time per interview room**. The Assessor now tells the manager instead of starting a second assessment.
3. **Timing came from the agents' own clocks.** The Assessor's times were close, but they didn't exactly match the message timestamps (for example, it recorded Task 1's answer at 20:34:30, but the message arrived at 20:34:27). We changed the Assessor and Interviewer to use **the chat's message timestamps**, and to write "time not available" rather than guess.

The fixes are in the current [`skillroom.template.yaml`](../skillroom.template.yaml) and [`agents/`](../agents) files. The next full run will add the Evaluator's scored report to this folder.

### What the timing shows

Amina answered each task well inside its time limit (1–4 minutes, against limits of 8–10). The Evaluator is told to note fast, polished answers as a timing signal, never as proof, and never to lower a skill score because of timing. In this run, the fast answers came from an AI playing the candidate. That is exactly the pattern the integrity check is meant to bring to a human's attention.
